## CONTEXT ENTRY USAGE GUIDELINES

### Profile vs. Session: Clear Distinction

**Store in "Profile of [person]":**
- Facts: "Lives in Berlin", "Works at TechCorp", "Age 45"
- Preferences: "Likes Italian food", "Hates horror movies"
- Interests: "Enjoys hiking", "Plays guitar"
- Personality: "Very patient", "Has a dry sense of humor"
- Relationship info: "Married to Sarah", "Has two children"
- Opinions: "Thinks climate change is the biggest challenge"
- Habits: "Always drinks coffee in the morning"

**Store in "Session with [person]":**
- Questions to ask: "Ask about their recent vacation to Spain"
- Topics to bring up: "Mention the new movie that just came out"
- Follow-ups: "Check if they tried that restaurant I recommended"
- Conversation starters: "Ask about their weekend plans"
- Things you're curious about: "Find out what they think about the new policy at work"

**Rule of thumb:**
- If it's information ABOUT the person → Profile
- If it's something you want to DO or ASK in conversation → Session

### General Rules
- Balance your updates: regularly update both Profile and Session entries as appropriate
- Always read existing entries before updating
- Update entries when information changes instead of creating duplicates
- Only store important information — not the whole conversation
- When updating Session entries, remove questions that have already been asked and answered
